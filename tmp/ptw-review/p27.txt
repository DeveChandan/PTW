4.5. LINE BREAKING: 
Line breaking refers to the controlled opening of piping, ductwork, or 
containment systems that hold hazardous materials. This activity is considered 
high-risk due to the potential release of chemicals, gases, or steam, which can 
pose serious hazards to personnel and the environment. 
This procedure applies to lines containing high pressure, high vacuum, 
corrosive, flammable, toxic, or hazardous fluids. Water supply, Process water, 
steam below 3 Kg/cm² and airline systems are excluded from line-breaking 
permits. 
First breaking/opening of the line must be done in presence of filed 
operator/process engineer.  
Examples of Line Breaking Activities 
• Removing bolts( even single bolt) from flanges 
• Opening valves to release material 
• Removing valve bonnets and check valves 
• Turning spectacle plates (blanks/blinds) 
• Disconnecting tubing or hoses 
• Penetrating a line by mechanical means 
• Opening inspection ports or sight glasses 
• Replacing gland packing on valves 
• Making the first cut on isolated pipelines 
Approver I & Approver II requirements: 
• PTW line breaking activities involving the following hazardous materials (but not limited 
to) require approval I & Approval II signature on PTW. 
1. Sulfuric Acid 
2. Chlorine 
3. Methanol 
4. Natural Gas 
5. Caustic Soda 
6. Hydrochloric Acid 
7. Anhydrous Hydrofluoric Acid 
8. Diethyl Ether (DHF) 
9. Chloroform 
10. Chlorodifluoromethane (R-22)
