• Confined Spaces include, but are not limited to: - tanks, vessels, vaults, certain pipelines, 
certain pits, elevated water towers, boilers, columns, reactors, spheres, silos, sumps, 
sewers, underground service and column skirts (those skirts where operator  goes for 
valve operations is excluded from the definition, provided there is cross ventilation and 
there are two opening for ingress and egress.)  
• Note that these spaces could reasonably have atmospheres that are oxygen deficient or 
contain other hazards, typical examples of confined space hazards. 
• These spaces are subjected to potential hazards, viz., accumulation of toxic or flammable 
gas, vapors, inert gas, oxygen deficient (<19.5%v/v), flooding of liquid, steam or any other 
hazardous substance or development of hazardous conditions. 
• A Work permit must be issued before a person enters a Confined Space, and before the 
issue of the Work permit, a formal documented P-JSA must take place.  
• The Work permit must specify details of the location and the type of work to be done, 
with the P-JSA attached or cross referenced, and with the necessary protective measures 
detailed or attached. 
• Use of Destress meter is also mandatory when person entering inside the confined space. 
4.3.1 Entry into a Confined Space must be in accordance with GFL Standard and a 
requirement demanded by legislation. Thus: 
 
No person shall enter a Confined Space unless:  
• Formal P-JSA has been completed. 
• All product and service lines entering the space are disconnected, blanked and 
checked. Drawing for the same is to be attached with the permit. 
• All mechanical equipment such as stirrers and heaters are positively disconnected and 
electrically locked off. 
• All appropriate operating and maintenance personnel are advised of the activity and 
the period for which it is approved. 
• All electric connection shall be provided with ELCB. 
• For jobs in confined spaces only 24 V Flame proof hand lamp to be used. If lighting is 
required for any job involving release of flammable vapors or work is carried out in a 
hazardous area, it is mandatory to use flameproof electrical equipment. Only 
flameproof 24 V electric hand lamp shall be used. Use of 230 V hand lamps inside 
confined space/vessel is prohibited. 
• When a person is entering confined space, the receiver must keep minimum one 
trained attendants at the manhole or entry point 
• Wherever possible, lifelines with safety harness should be used for entry into 
confined space
