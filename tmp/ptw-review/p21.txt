The Work at height permit issuer is requires to ensure that: 
• A P-JSA is carried out for all work conducted at height. 
• People working at a height are medical fit. 
• Equipment used for work at height is properly inspected and maintained. 
• Risks from fragile surfaces are properly controlled.  
• The P -JSA should include a careful examination of what harm could be caused from 
working at height with a view to taking the effective steps to reduce the likelihood of this 
harm occurring, either through avoiding the activity or, whe re this is not reasonably 
practicable, by carrying it out in a safe manner using work equipment that is appropriate 
to the task and the level of risk. 
General Medical fitness is prerequisite for the persons performing all the job at height. 
In additions the following must be done: 
• Hypertension / High blood pressure 
• Epilepsy fits (Past History of illness) 
• Vertigo/Height Phobia 
 
Employees (including Contractors) who have not undergone additional medical check -up, 
required for ‘Work at Height’ must not be deployed on height work. 
 
Medical tests performed for Work at Height is valid for in six months. 
                      
Refer to the GFL “Work at Height” Standard for details of mandatory requirements and safeguards of 
working at Height.
