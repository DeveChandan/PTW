8.3 Permit Acceptor (Person who is responsible to execute work)  
• The permit acceptor is normally Company staff but in certain instances (e.g., in a project 
site) may be the contractor.  
• There are many activities that will be undertaken where the contractor is more of an 
expert than Company staff.  
• It is not the purpose of the PTW system, or the Work Permit, to advise the contractor 
how to specifically address the expertise issues in their scope of work – the contractor 
will address this via completion of the P-JSA.  
• The permit, when combined with a P-JSA, and contractor expertise, ensures that EHS 
risks involved in the work are addressed.  
• Permit acceptor shall be responsible for executing the entire work in full compliance 
with the permit requirements.  
 
               His/her responsibilities include the following:  
• Give tool box talk to permit holder and manpower involve in work. Mention their name 
in Permit and take signature.  
• Understanding the potential hazards  
• Understanding how the hazards are controlled. 
• Knowing the emergency situations likely to occur in the work area and actions to be 
taken including evacuation procedure.  
• Ensuring that the permit holder understands all the hazards and controls, will explain 
them to the work group, and will ensure they are maintained throughout the work.  
• Informing all people who may be directly affected by the work.  
• Signing and accepting the permit.  
• Knowing the status of the work at all times,  
• Transferring duties formally
