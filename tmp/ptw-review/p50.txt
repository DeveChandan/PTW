REMARKS 
A PRE-WORK INSPECTION & PREPARATION: 
01 Is the area free of flammable or combustible materials?   
02 Is firefighting equipment, including fire extinguishers (ABC 9 Kg), 
readily available, sufficient, and operational near the work location.   
03 Are the involved equipment (e.g., welding machine, gas cutting set, 
tools) in good condition, with no damage or defects   
04 
Is the LEL meter/multi -gas detector calibrated and functional? The 
normal LEL value of flammable vapor/ hazardous gases should be zero 
(Hazardous area) and never exceed 5%. LEL should be check within 2 
hrs frequency. (In flameproof area) 
  
05 Has the gas cylinder checklist been approved by the acceptor, and are 
the cylinders kept in a trolley   
06 Are earthing connection tight at electrical extension box, machine, and 
welding objects?   
07 Is the mitigation plan for the task available for emergencies and 
communicated (ensure it is covered in the P-JSA)   
09 Is the welding machine on/off switch available near the work area to 
cut off power in case of an emergency?   
10 Is the welding machine frame grounded?   
11 Are welding or thermal cutting performing in confined spaces, gas 
cylinders and welding machines kept outside?   
B TOOL BOX TALK (TBT): 
01 Gas lines purged individually before lighting the torch.   
02 Oxygen valve closed when lighting acetylene (DA Gas).   
03 The welder to avoid coiling or looping the welding electrode cable 
around parts of their body?   
C AREA PREPARATION: 
01 Hot work area should be free from combustible material at least 25 ft 
radius distance.   
02 If the area cannot be free from combustibles, is it covered with fire -
resistant materials (e.g., fire blanket)?   
03 Are workplace floors swept clean of combustible debris?
