Self-Permitting: 
  In a Permit to Work (PTW) system, self-permitting refers to the practice where an individual 
authorizes their own work permit without requiring approval from a separate, designated authority. ( 
e.g Mr. XYZ is issuing the permit to Mr XYZ for Hot Work) 
Isolation: 
A physical barrier between a source of energy (mechanical, electrical, hydraulic or static head) or 
process materials. 
Live Permit: 
A permit for a work that is active and ongoing. 
Method Statement: 
A breakdown of an activity into individual tasks. 
Monitoring:  
Routine checking of permits and controls in the course of the work. 
Revalidation: 
Reassessing the work site and PTW controls to determine if work can continue safely for another set 
period of time. 
Review: 
Examining the fundamental design of the system to see whether it should be changed in the light of 
experience. 
Shift Handover: 
Process of transferring responsibility for controlling and monitoring work from the present Operations 
shift to the oncoming shift. 
Supporting Certificate Signatory: 
The person responsible for confirming that the hazards have been (Validated, Authorized Safety 
correctly identified and assessed and, that the proposed controls area inspector, Fire Permit 
Signatory) adequate. 
Suspension:  
Temporary invalidation of a permit for a period during which the work may not proceed. 
Validity Period: 
The period during which a permit may remain active, and after which the work site controls must be 
reassessed. 
Worker (User): 
 A member of the work party, working to the instruction of the Permit Holder.
