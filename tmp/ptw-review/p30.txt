4.7. RADIOGRAPHY JOB REQUIREMENTS AND PERMITS: 
• The radiography should preferably be carried out at a time, when occupancy is least. 
• The supervisor must be available at site during the radiography work. 
• Although radiography jobs do not qualify as hot jobs by definition, they still require a 
hot work permit due to the involvement of radioactive sources. 
 
                           Before issuing permitting to start Radiography, ensure: 
• TLD badge should be available with Radiographer 
• Availability of Dosimeter and Radiation level Survey meter and its type  
• Signage display 
• Area barricading according to safe distance required 
• Warning signal  
• Information to surrounding plant and no other activities allowed in surrounding area. 
                     Work Involving Radioactive Sources: 
• The isolation or restoration of a radioactive source, including the use of mobile X -
ray/radiography equipment.  
• For radiography jobs on pipelines or equipment, the isolation and restoration of the 
radioactive source are essential.  
• A separate radiation permit must be issued for works related to radiography.
