14. Frequently asked Question and Answer: -  
1. What is the primary purpose of the Permit to Work (PTW) system? 
a) To ensure work is completed quickly 
b) To align with corporate standards and statutory requirements 
c) To reduce paperwork 
d) To increase production efficiency

a) Permit Acceptor + Permit Issuer 
b) Permit Holder+ Unit Head 
c) Permit Issuer+ Approver 
d) Unit Head + Approver 
3. What must be confirmed by the Permit Issuer before authorizing work to proceed? 
a) Availability of tools 
b) Correct identification of hazards and adequacy of controls 
c) Number of workers 
d) Duration of the work 
4. Which document must be prepared by the team before completing the PTW? 
a) Safety Data Sheet (SDS) 
b) Job Safety Analysis (JSA) 
c) Risk rating 
d) Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) 
5. What is the validity period for a Cold Work Permit? 
a) 24 hours 
b) 7 days 
c) 8 hours or shift end, whichever is earlier 
d) 1 month 
6. What must be done if the work conditions change during the course of an activity under permit? 
a) Continue working as planned 
b) Refer back to the Permit Issuer 
c) Inform the Unit Head 
d) Stop work immediately 
7. What is required for any maintenance, installation, and repair work on electrical equipment? 
a) Hot Work Permit 
b) Cold Work Permit 
c) Electrical Work Permit 
d) Confined Space Entry Permit 
8. Who is responsible for carrying out gas tests before and during jobs? 
a) Permit Holder
