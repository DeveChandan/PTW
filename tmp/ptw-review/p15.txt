For Confined Space Entry:  
1. Entry to the confined to made within 1 hrs. of gas testing otherwise conduct gas testing 
again. 
2. A periodic gas testing, using portable gas testing instrument to be done, every 2 hrs. 
3. It is suggested If Continuous gas monitor is available is to be used inside the confined 
space to quickly detect the change in atmospheric conditions inside the Confined Space. 
                      Oxygen concentration:  
• Wherever forced ventilation is used. The gas test should be carried out after 
minimum 10 min of stopping the educator/fan/blower.  
• Gas testing in confined spaces must be revalidated before reentry if there has 
been a break of more than one hour. 
• If there is any abnormality found the work must be stopped immediately and 
inform the permit issuer and responsible person.  
    NOTE: 
• Carry out regular gas test of confined spaces, where there is possibility of gas 
generation due to nature of work, being performed inside, such as removal of 
sludge, hot works, etc.
