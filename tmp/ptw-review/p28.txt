 Responsibilities of Line Owner 
➢ Lockout or Isolation: Ensure isolation from supplier and receiver sides, tagging 
and locking. 
➢ Hazard Identification: Assess toxicity, flammability, pressure, temperature, and 
secondary energy sources. 
➢ Draining and Decontamination: Drain low points, vent high points, flush and 
purge lines. 
➢ Tagging and Isolation: Tag valves and blinds; keep valves open to avoid trapping 
material. 
➢ Permit Coordination: Issue permit from safety district owner. 
➢ Job Turnover: Review job scope before and after work. 
 
System Preparation (Clearing & Verification) 
• Drain low points and vent high points 
• Flush or purge lines until hazards are reduced 
• Verify cleanliness and absence of trapped pressure 
• Stop work if hazards persist 
Job Planning Details 
• Hazard list and isolation points 
• Flushing and clearing steps
