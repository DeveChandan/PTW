Permit issuer: 
A Permit Issuer is a person who is engineer/ shift In-charge, has experience of total one year plus 3-
month experience in same plant. He has got trained, (PTW standard, JSA Standard, & ERP), 
competent, and authorized to issue a Permit to Work as per permit matrix Annexure format. Has gone 
through MOC(P). 
Note: matrix approved by Plant HOD. 
Permit Acceptor: 
A person who is an engineer accepting the permit. Person who is responsible to handle work. He/She 
shall have experience of total 1 year plus 3 months in same plant. Is trained (PTW standard, JSA 
Standard, & ERP), competent, and authorized to accept a Permit to Work as per permit matrix. 
Annexure format. Has gone through MOC(P). 
Note:  PTW matrix approved by Plant HOD. 
Permit Approver-I: 
A person who is L4 (Manager) and above level, can approve the permit as Approve-I. He/ She shall 
have minimum experience of total 5 years including 3 months in the same area/plant. He/ She has to 
complete the self-study of training module of PTW , JSA, CSE, Hot Work and ERP . Has gone through 
MOCP Process. He can approve all the permits of plant/area/equipment which are under his 
jurisdiction. 
Note: PTW matrix approved by Dy Unit Head/Unit Head. 
Permit Approver-II: 
A person who is L3 (Plant HOD level) can approve the permit as Approver-II. He/ She shall have 
experience of 3 months in same plant/area. He/ She has to complete the self-study of training module 
of PTW, JSA, CSE, Hot Work and ERP . He should have gone through MOCP process. He can approve all 
the permit of plant/area/equipment which are under his jurisdiction. 
On w/off, Holiday or non-availability of Approver I, He can approve all the permit of 
plant/area/equipment which are under his jurisdiction as Approver I & Approver II. 
Note:  PTW matrix approved by Dy Unit Head/Unit Head. 
Permit Approver-III : 
A person who is L2 (VP/AVP/Sr.VP) can approve the permit as Approver-III. He/ She shall have 
experience of 3 months in same plant/area. He/ She has to complete the self-study of training module 
of PTW, JSA, CSE, Hot Work and ERP . He should have gone through MOCP process.
