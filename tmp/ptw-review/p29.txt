Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) 
Minimum                               : safety shoes, helmet, splash goggles, face shield. 
Chemical/Corrosive/Toxic  : chemical-resistant suit, gloves, boots, SCBA if required. 
Flammable/Explosive         : flame-resistant suit, nitrile gloves, SCBA as needed. 
Steam/Nitrogen/Gas         : face shield, leather gloves, SCBA for oxygen-deficient 
areas. 
Job Execution 
• Barricade work area 
• Verify safety shower operability 
• Perform initial break away from line of fire 
• Loosen bottom bolts slowly 
• Use secondary containment 
• Stop and consult operations if plugs or pressure are found 
Box-up and Return to Service 
• Reassemble with correct bolts and gaskets 
• Inspect integrity and cleanliness 
• Remove isolation as per PTW closure 
• Conduct turnover review and document sign-offs 
 
Refer to the GFL “Line Breaking” Standard  & Site procedure for details. 
 
4.6 EXCAVATION ACTIVITIES: 
• For any excavation activity 30 cm or deeper, obtain signatures from Electrical, 
Instrumentation, Utility, and HSE departments to confirm the absence of underground 
utilities in the marked area. 
• A one-time approval is required for the same excavation area, and the approved permit 
must be attached to the next day's permit. 
• If any underground utility is present in marked area, then concern dept. must written in 
permit & confirm manual / Mechanical Excavation on permit. 
• Excavation area should be free from fumes/gases heavier than air because it may cause 
suffocation or poisoning.  
• All excavation of depth more than 1.2 m or more should be treated as confined space and 
permit for entry into confined space should be obtained.
