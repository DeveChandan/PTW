4.8 RIGGING JOBS: 
• Pre-Job Meeting:  Conduct a meeting with all personnel involved in the rigging job to 
discuss the plan, potential hazards, and emergency procedures 
• Equipment Inspection: Inspect all rigging equipment, including cranes, slings, and hooks, 
to ensure they are in good working condition and meet safety standards 
• Execution of Work: Perform the rigging job as per the approved plan, ensuring all safety 
measures are followed. Continuous monitoring is essential to address any issues that arise 
• Post-Job Review: After completing the job, conduct a review to identify any incidents or 
near-misses and document lessons learned for future reference. 
• Rigging Plan must be approved as per checklist HSE/SAF/F/27  
•  i) For load ≤15 MT rigging plan should be approval by Mainte nance Engineer, 
Maintenance Manager and Maintenance HOD. 
• ii) For load > 15 MT rigging plan should be approval by Maintenance Engineer, 
Maintenance Manager, Maintenance HOD, and CES / Project head. 
• iii) For load > 30 MT rigging plan should be approval by Ma intenance Engineer, 
Maintenance Manager, Maintenance HOD, CES / Project head and Engineering Head.
