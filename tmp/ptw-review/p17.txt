3.8 Permit Validity, Extension and Approver: 
 
• All permit to be revalidated during every shift change and has to sign on the permit. 
• Permit to be revalidate if any issuer, operator or acceptor change during work and has to 
sign on permit.   
• All permits issued by the A -shift engineer need to be extended before 15:45 hrs. B - Shift 
team has to sign the permit. 
• All permits issued by the G shift engineer that need to be extended after 17:40 Hrs, must 
be revalidated and signed by the B shift engineer within one Hrs. 
• If both the Head of Department (HOD) and the assigned L4 person are not available (during 
w/off /Holiday), PTW will be approved b y SDO/NDO after obtaining approval from the 
concerned plant HOD via email.
